05 / Questions

Straight answers.

Is this really once-off?

Yes. You pay once, you own it. The only recurring cost is your domain (R150/year). Skip the care plan if you want to handle renewals yourself.

What does the care plan cover?

Domain renewal, uptime monitoring, and two free content edits a year. Optional but recommended.

Can I edit it myself?

Yes. You get a phone-friendly dashboard. Change prices, add products, upload photos, mark orders paid and shipped. No code.

What if I want to leave?

You own everything — code, domain, data, customers. I hand it over cleanly. No lock-in, ever.

Who am I talking to?

Me. Not an agency. Not a team. A solo developer who built this system and answers his own WhatsApp. You'll talk to the same person from first message to handover.
